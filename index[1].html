Our Mission

To provide financial services to the poorest of the poor, empowering them to improve their quality of life through responsible lending, savings, and financial literacy programs across the Philippines.

35+ Years of Service

We believe every Filipino deserves access to financial tools that can transform their lives. Since 1988, ASA Philippines has been at the forefront of microfinance, reaching the most underserved communities nationwide.

🎯

Poverty Alleviation

Targeted programs designed specifically for low-income households and micro-entrepreneurs.

📚

Financial Literacy

We educate clients on responsible borrowing, savings habits, and sustainable financial planning.

🤝

Community Development

Building stronger communities through inclusive economic participation and social empowerment.
